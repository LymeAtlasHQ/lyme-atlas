# 🧬 LymeWire

> Open science, AI-assisted research, patient support, and evidence navigation for Lyme and tick-borne disease.

## Vision

Build the most trusted, evidence-based Lyme disease knowledge and support ecosystem across web, iOS, Android, Telegram, and shared data services.

## Mission

Provide free, continuously updated, scientifically grounded tools and information for patients, clinicians, researchers, and collaborators.

## Product ecosystem

- LymeWire Patient App — web, iOS, and Android.
- LymeWire AI — evidence-aware research and support assistant.
- LymeWire Telegram — conversational access to research, care navigation, and patient support.
- LymeWire Admin — internal operations, content, and system management.
- LymeWire Business — sales, partnerships, revenue, and lead tracking.

## Core Principles

- Evidence before opinion.
- Transparency.
- Open science.
- Continuous improvement.
- Patient-centered.
- AI-assisted, human-reviewed.

## Brand

Primary name: **LymeWire**

Core idea: **Find the signal.**

`LymeAtlasHQ` and `Lyme Atlas` are legacy project/repository names and should not be presented as the company brand.
