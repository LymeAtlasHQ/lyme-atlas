# Contributing to Lyme Atlas

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Lyme Atlas.

Our goal is to build the world's most trusted, evidence-based knowledge platform for Lyme disease and other tick-borne illnesses.

## Before Contributing

Please read:

- README.md
- MISSION.md
- SCIENTIFIC_STANDARDS.md
- ETHICS.md

## Contribution Principles

Every contribution should:

- Be evidence-based.
- Include reliable references whenever possible.
- Respect scientific uncertainty.
- Use clear and understandable language.
- Improve knowledge for patients and professionals alike.

## Preferred Sources

- PubMed
- Cochrane Library
- ClinicalTrials.gov
- CDC
- NIH
- WHO
- Peer-reviewed journals

## Reporting Issues

If you find incorrect information, outdated references, or missing evidence, please open an Issue.

## Our Philosophy

We value curiosity over certainty.

We welcome respectful discussion.

We improve through evidence.

Thank you for helping Lyme Atlas grow.
