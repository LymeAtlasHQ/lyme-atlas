# Scientific Standards

## Our Principles

Every statement in Lyme Atlas should be supported by the best available scientific evidence.

## Evidence Hierarchy

Highest priority:

1. Systematic Reviews
2. Meta-Analyses
3. Clinical Practice Guidelines
4. Randomized Controlled Trials
5. Cohort Studies
6. Case-Control Studies
7. Case Reports

## Preferred Sources

- PubMed
- Cochrane Library
- ClinicalTrials.gov
- CDC
- NIH
- WHO
- IDSA
- ESCMID

## Citation Policy

Every medical claim should include a reliable scientific reference whenever possible.

## Transparency

When evidence is conflicting or insufficient, Lyme Atlas will clearly state the uncertainty instead of presenting speculation as fact.
