# Ethics

## Our Ethical Principles

Lyme Atlas is committed to scientific integrity, transparency, and respect for every patient.

## We Believe

- Patients deserve respect.
- Uncertainty should never be hidden.
- Scientific disagreement should remain respectful.
- Every claim should be supported by evidence whenever possible.

## AI Ethics

Artificial intelligence is a tool to assist learning and research.

AI must never replace clinical judgment.

Generated content should always be reviewed against scientific literature.

## Community

We welcome respectful discussion.

We reject harassment, discrimination, and misinformation.

## Our Promise

If we discover that published information is incorrect, we will correct it transparently.

Truth matters more than being right.
